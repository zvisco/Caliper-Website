Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

Caliper does not collect, transmit or share any data.

Your images and measurements

Images you open in Caliper, and the measurements you make on them, are stored only on your Mac in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded, synced or sent to the developer or anyone else. Caliper makes no network connections.

What is not collected

  • No account or sign-in
  • No analytics, usage statistics or crash reporting by the developer
  • No advertising and no tracking
  • No third-party software development kits

Apple may provide the developer with anonymous, aggregated sales and crash information for apps downloaded from the App Store, according to your Mac's sharing settings. That is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Files you choose

Caliper can read only the image files you select or paste, and can write only the exports you choose to save.

Deleting your data

You can delete any saved X-ray, or all of them, from the Saved X-rays page inside the app. Deleting the app removes its stored data.

Patient information and HIPAA

Caliper is not intended for storing protected health information (PHI), and the developer is not a HIPAA covered entity or business associate: no image or measurement ever reaches the developer. Images saved in the app are protected only by your Mac's own security, such as your login password and FileVault; the app adds no encryption of its own.

De-identify images before opening, exporting or sharing them. You are responsible for following HIPAA and any other rules or organizational policies that apply to you. If you email for support, please do not include images that identify a patient.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
