Support

Caliper has a built-in guide: choose Help in the toolbar, or Help ▸ Caliper Help in the menu bar. If that doesn't answer your question, email zviscodev@gmail.com and include your macOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened. Please don't send images that identify a patient.

Getting started

  1. Click Open and choose an image, or copy one and press ⌘V.
  2. Pick a tool: Angle, Cobb, Line or Osteotomy.
  3. Click on the image to place points. Drag a point to adjust it; drag a label to move it out of the way.
  4. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag the image to pan, and click Fit to see the whole film.
  5. Click Export to save an annotated PNG.

Frequently asked questions

Which image formats can I open?

PNG, JPEG, and the other common image formats your Mac can display. DICOM files aren't read directly; export the view as an image from your viewer, or take a screenshot and paste it.

Can I measure real distances in millimetres?

No. Angles are exact for the points you place, but line lengths are shown in image pixels. Caliper does not calibrate for magnification.

Where are my X-rays saved?

On your Mac only, inside the app's private storage. Nothing is uploaded or synced. Open the Open menu and choose All saved X-rays to see, rename or delete them.

How do I delete everything?

Go to Open ▸ All saved X-rays and choose Delete all. Removing the app from your Mac also removes its stored data.

How do I plan an osteotomy?

Choose Osteotomy, click the hinge end of the cut, then the opposite cortex. Drag the round knob, or the Opening slider on the right, to open or close the wedge. In the Osteotomy tab you can swap which side moves, or press Window to outline the part of the image that should move.

Can I undo a mistake?

Yes: click Undo or press ⌘Z. To remove a single measurement, use the button beside it in the side panel.

The exported image is larger than the X-ray. Why?

When an osteotomy hinges a fragment or a label outside the original frame, the export grows so nothing is cropped.

How do I rotate or flip an X-ray?

Use the four buttons under the Brightness and Contrast sliders: rotate left, rotate right, flip left-to-right and flip top-to-bottom. Everything you've drawn turns with the image and the angles stay the same.

Is Caliper HIPAA compliant?

Caliper is not intended for storing protected health information. Nothing leaves your Mac, but the app adds no encryption of its own, so de-identify images before opening or sharing them. You are responsible for following HIPAA and your organization's policies.

Can I use Caliper for clinical decisions?

Caliper is for reference and education only. It is not a medical device and not a certified diagnostic tool, and should not be the sole basis for diagnosis or treatment.
